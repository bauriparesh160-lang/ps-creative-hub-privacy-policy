Privacy Policy

PS Creative Hub
Effective date: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PS Creative Hub handles information when you use the PS Creative Hub Android application.

PS Creative Hub is designed with an offline-first approach. Most creative work, projects, assets, editing and exports are processed and stored locally on your device.

Developer/Publisher: Ram Bauri

Application: PS Creative Hub

2. Information and Content You Provide

Depending on the features you use, PS Creative Hub may process content that you intentionally provide or import, including:

  • Photos and images selected from your device.
  • Video clips selected from your device.
  • Audio files selected from your device.
  • Voice-over recordings made using the microphone.
  • Designs, projects and canvas information.
  • Text, prompts and other content entered into AI tools.
  • AI-generated creations saved within the application.
  • Custom fonts such as TTF or OTF files that you import.
  • Assets such as logos, backgrounds, stickers and other creative files.

3. Microphone and Voice Recordings

PS Creative Hub requests microphone permission only when you intentionally use the voice-over recording feature.

Voice recordings are processed locally and are stored in the application's private cache/storage area. They are not automatically uploaded to a server.

A temporary recording can be discarded by the user. Cached files may remain until they are removed by the application, cleared from cache, or reclaimed by the operating system.

4. Photos, Videos and Other Media

Photos, videos and audio files selected through Android's media pickers or file chooser are normally processed locally on your device.

PS Creative Hub does not automatically upload your personal media simply because you import it into the application.

If you intentionally use an AI feature that requires an online AI provider, the selected content may be transmitted to that provider as described in the AI section below.

5. AI Features and Google Generative Language API

PS Creative Hub can operate in an offline/no-API mode. In this mode, supported AI-related text features use local fallback logic and do not require an online AI request.

If an AI provider is configured and you intentionally use an online AI feature, the information required for that request may be sent to Google's Generative Language API.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Your AI text prompt.
  • A selected image used for an AI image operation.
  • Other information required to process the requested AI operation.

AI requests are made only when you intentionally trigger an online AI operation. PS Creative Hub does not automatically send all of your device content to Google.

Google may process information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Local Projects and Database

Designs, projects and related creative information are stored locally on your device using the application's local database/storage.

PS Creative Hub does not currently provide automatic cloud synchronization of your projects.

7. AI History and Saved Creations

AI prompts and generated creations that you choose to save are stored locally within the application.

AI prompt history is limited by the application and can be cleared using the available AI history controls.

8. Custom Fonts and Assets

Custom TTF/OTF fonts imported by you are stored in the application's private storage and are used locally for creative work.

Asset Library items such as images, logos, backgrounds and stickers are stored locally and can be removed by you using the available controls.

9. Exported Content

When you export content, the resulting files are saved according to the application's Android storage/export implementation.

Images and other image-based exports may be saved through Android's MediaStore in the Pictures/PSCreativeHub location. Video exports are stored in the application's internal export storage and can be shared through Android's secure FileProvider mechanism.

You control whether and where you share exported files using Android's sharing features.

10. Sharing with Other Services

PS Creative Hub does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to an online AI provider only when you intentionally use an AI feature that requires such a service.

If you use Android's sharing functionality, the content you choose to share may be provided to the application or service you select.

11. Firebase and Analytics

The application project may contain Firebase-related dependencies, but PS Creative Hub does not currently use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics for routine collection of your personal data.

PS Creative Hub does not currently use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics for user profiling.

12. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

PS Creative Hub does not intentionally collect the following through its current application functionality:

  • Precise location.
  • Contacts or contact lists.
  • Calendar information.
  • Phone call information.
  • Advertising ID for profiling.
  • Device serial numbers.
  • Passwords or account credentials.

13. Permissions

The current application may request:

  • Internet: Used for online AI requests when you intentionally use an online AI feature.
  • Microphone: Used only when you intentionally record a voice-over.

The microphone capability is optional and is not required for the application to function as a whole.

14. Security

PS Creative Hub uses Android application sandboxing and secure HTTPS communication for supported online network requests.

API credentials, when configured, are not intentionally displayed in the application's normal user interface or logs.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored projects, assets and saved creations generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear application data, or uninstall the application.

You can delete supported individual projects, designs, assets, recordings and AI creations using the application's available controls.

Android system controls can also be used to clear the application's stored data.

16. Children's Privacy

PS Creative Hub is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through a child-directed service.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, data practices or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will be published at this same public Privacy Policy URL, with the effective date updated when appropriate.
